Here’s a **comprehensive breakdown of a Municipal Corporation Management System** — suitable for a web-based SaaS or government-grade software.
It covers **all key departments, workflows, and digital governance features**, designed for transparency, citizen engagement, and administrative efficiency.

---

## 🏛️ Municipal Corporation Management System

**Goal:**
Digitize and automate the operations of city municipal bodies — improving service delivery, citizen engagement, tax collection, waste management, and infrastructure tracking.

---

## ⚙️ 1. Core Modules & Features

### 1️⃣ Citizen Management

- **Citizen Registration & Login**

  - Aadhaar / Mobile / Email verification
  - Profile management (address, ward, zone)

- **Citizen Dashboard**

  - Track applications, bills, complaints, approvals
  - Notifications & reminders

- **Grievance / Complaint Management**

  - Submit issue by category (sanitation, street light, water supply, etc.)
  - Upload image, video, or location
  - Ticket tracking with escalation matrix
  - Auto-routing to concerned department
  - Feedback & satisfaction rating

---

### 2️⃣ Property & Tax Management

- **Property Registration & Assessment**

  - GIS mapping integration
  - Ownership details, size, and valuation

- **Property Tax Calculation**

  - Auto-calculation based on ward, area, building type, and usage

- **Online Payment**

  - Integration with Razorpay / Paytm / BharatPe Gateway

- **Defaulter List & Notices**

  - Auto-reminder via SMS/Email
  - Penalty & interest computation

- **Tax Reports**

  - Collection analytics by ward / region

---

### 3️⃣ Trade License & Business Permits

- **New License Application**

  - Document upload & verification (PAN, GST, Shop Act)

- **Renewal / Expiry Alerts**
- **Inspection Scheduling & Approval Workflow**
- **License Fee Payment & Digital Certificate**

---

### 4️⃣ Building Plan Approval System (BPAS)

  - Biodegradable / Non-biodegradable / Hazardous waste

- **Vendor & Contractor Management**

  - Contracts, performance reports

- **Citizen Feedback & Rating System**

### 6️⃣ Water Supply & Drainage

## 🧭 Workflow Overview

### Citizen Workflow

1. Citizen registers → verifies identity
2. Submits request (tax payment / complaint / permit)
3. System assigns it to concerned department automatically
4. Department staff processes → updates status → citizen notified
5. Citizen gives feedback → record logged in analytics

---

### Admin / Officer Workflow

1. Officer logs in via role-based access
2. Dashboard shows pending applications & performance KPIs
3. Approves / rejects / assigns tasks
4. Generates reports for higher authorities
5. Data synced for transparency & audit
